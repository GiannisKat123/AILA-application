# AILA – Artificial Intelligence-driven Framework and Legal Advice Tools for Phishing Prevention and Mitigation in Information Systems

## Project Overview
AILA stands for **“Artificial Intelligence-driven Framework and Legal Advice Tools for Phishing Prevention and Mitigation in Information Systems.”** It is a research project financially supported by the **Hellenic Foundation for Research and Innovation (H.F.R.I.)**.

The project falls within the thematic area of **“Social Sciences, Arts & Humanities, Management & Economic Innovation”**, and specifically within the thematic field of **“Development of Innovative Applications and Sustainable Business Models.”**  

The main objective of AILA is the development of an **innovative multidisciplinary framework** that addresses phishing cyberattacks. By doing so, the project provides groundbreaking opportunities to enhance the sustainability of business operations in critical domains, such as e-banking, e-government, and e-education.  

---

## Academic Coordination
The project is coordinated by the **Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering of the University of Patras (Greece)**, with external collaboration from the **Law School of the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (Greece)**.  

---

## Software Documentation
This repository contains the official software of the AILA project. The software is a **chatbot-like application** specifically designed for lawyers, legal practitioners, and the general public, with a particular focus on cybersecurity and phishing in the European Union and Greece.  

The chatbot is capable of providing **real-time answers in multiple languages**, including English and Greek. This release represents a **beta version**, which leaves room for future improvements and adaptations.  

### Key Features
- Real-time interactive chatbot functionality.  
- Multilingual support in English and Greek.  
- Integration of legal and cybersecurity knowledge.  
- Emphasis on phishing prevention and mitigation.  
- Research-driven prototype currently available as a beta version.  

---

## Repository Contents
The GitHub repository includes both the **frontend** and **backend** components of the AILA software:  
- **Frontend:** A web-based application for interacting with the chatbot.  
- **Backend:** The core services supporting multilingual processing, retrieval, and legal reasoning.  

The documentation provided in this repository will guide users through:  
- Installation and usage of the frontend.  
- Deployment and configuration of the backend.  
- Example queries and demonstration cases.  
- Future directions for research and software development.
